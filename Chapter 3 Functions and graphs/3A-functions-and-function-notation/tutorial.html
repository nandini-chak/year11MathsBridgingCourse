Theory · key ideas

A function is a machine. One input goes in. One output comes out.

  • Rule: y is a function of x when the value of y is completely fixed once you know x.
  • Names: x is independent. y is dependent.
  • Notation: f(x) means “the output of the rule f when the input is x”. It is not f times x.
  • Table: pick a few inputs and write the matching outputs beside them.
  • Restriction: if the story only allows certain numbers (whole numbers, or times from 0 to 30 minutes), write that restriction next to the formula.

Worked mini-examples

Example · put a number in

Quick fact: f(a) means replace every x in the rule with a, then calculate.

Step 1 · Rule: f(x)=x^2+5x. For f(3), replace x with 3: 3^2+5\times 3=9+15=24.

Step 2 · Same method: f(0)=0^2+5\times 0=0.

Step 3 · Same method: f(-3)=(-3)^2+5\times(-3)=9-15=-6. The square of -3 is 9, not -9.

Example · words into a formula

Step 1 · “Cube the number and subtract 7” means g(x)=x^3-7.

Step 2 · Table for x=-2,-1,0,1,2: outputs -15,-8,-7,-6,1. Check one: (-2)^3-7=-8-7=-15.

Example · put an expression in

Formula: (a+h)^2=a^2+2ah+h^2. The middle term is twice the product of the two pieces inside the bracket.

Step 1 · f(x)=x^2+5. Then f(a)=a^2+5. Nothing to expand.

Step 2 · f(a+1)=(a+1)^2+5=a^2+2a+1+5=a^2+2a+6.

Step 3 · Same method: f(a+h)=(a+h)^2+5=a^2+2ah+h^2+5.

Example · a restriction from the story

Step 1 · A snail climbs a 3 m wall at 10 cm each minute. 10 cm is 0.1 m, so the height after x minutes is y=\dfrac{1}{10}x metres.

Step 2 · Time to the top: 3\div 0.1=30 minutes. The snail starts at the bottom and stops at the top, so 0\le x\le 30.
