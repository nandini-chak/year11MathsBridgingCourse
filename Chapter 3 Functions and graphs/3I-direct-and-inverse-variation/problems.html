y varies directly with x.

(a) Write this algebraically. Use k for the constant of proportionality.

(b) Find k for each pair.

(i) y=10 when x=2

(ii) y=-6 when x=3

(iii) y=6 when x=4

Formula: “Varies directly” means y=kx. Here k is the fixed multiplier. It is not zero.

There is no pair yet, so k stays as a letter.

y=kx.

Formula: y=kx. In this part k is the fixed multiplier. The pair is y=10 when x=2, so k=y/x.

Step 1 · Put the pair into the formula.

10=k\times 2

Step 2 · Divide both sides by 2.

k=\dfrac{10}{2}=5

Step 3 · The rule for this pair is y=5x.

k=5.

Same formula: y=kx. This part uses the pair y=-6 when x=3, so k=y/x.

Step 1 · Put the pair into the formula.

-6=k\times 3

Step 2 · Divide both sides by 3. A negative divided by a positive stays negative.

k=\dfrac{-6}{3}=-2

Step 3 · The rule for this pair is y=-2x.

k=-2.

Same formula: y=kx. This part uses the pair y=6 when x=4, so k=y/x.

Step 1 · Put the pair into the formula.

6=k\times 4

Step 2 · Divide both sides by 4.

k=\dfrac{6}{4}=\dfrac{3}{2}

Step 3 · The rule for this pair is y=\dfrac{3}{2}x.

k=\dfrac{3}{2}.

2 · Exercise 3I Foundation

y is inversely proportional to x. That is the same as “varies inversely”.

(a) Write this algebraically. Use k for the constant of proportionality.

(b) Find k for each pair.

(i) y=2 when x=3

(ii) y=\dfrac{1}{2} when x=-3

(iii) y=-2 when x=4

Formula: “Varies inversely” means y=k/x. Here k is the fixed product of x and y.

y=\dfrac{k}{x}.

Formula: y=k/x. In this part k is the fixed product. The pair is y=2 when x=3, so k=xy.

Step 1 · Put the pair into the formula.

2=\dfrac{k}{3}

Step 2 · Multiply both sides by 3.

k=2\times 3=6

Step 3 · The rule for this pair is y=6/x.

k=6.

Same formula: y=k/x. This part uses y=\dfrac{1}{2} when x=-3, so k=xy.

Step 1 · Put the pair into the formula.

\dfrac{1}{2}=\dfrac{k}{-3}

Step 2 · Multiply both sides by -3.

k=\dfrac{1}{2}\times(-3)=-\dfrac{3}{2}

Positive times negative is negative.

Step 3 · The rule for this pair is y=-\dfrac{3}{2x}.

k=-\dfrac{3}{2}.

Same formula: y=k/x. This part uses y=-2 when x=4, so k=xy.

Step 1 · Put the pair into the formula.

-2=\dfrac{k}{4}

Step 2 · Multiply both sides by 4.

k=-2\times 4=-8

Step 3 · The rule for this pair is y=-8/x.

k=-8.

3 · Exercise 3I Foundation

(a) y is in direct proportion with x. What happens to y when x is:

(i) doubled?   (ii) halved?

(b) y varies inversely with x. What happens to y when x is:

(i) doubled?   (ii) halved?

Formula: Direct proportion means y=kx. k stays the same when x changes.

Step 1 · The new x is 2x.

Step 2 · Put that into the formula.

y_{\text{new}}=k\times(2x)=2(kx)=2y

Step 3 · Check with a pair. If y=12 when x=3, then k=4. Double x to 6:

y=4\times 6=24

24 is double 12.

y doubles.

Same formula: y=kx. k stays fixed.

Step 1 · The new x is x/2.

Step 2 · Put that into the formula.

y_{\text{new}}=k\times\dfrac{x}{2}=\dfrac{1}{2}(kx)=\dfrac{1}{2}y

Step 3 · Same check pair: k=4, y=12 when x=3. Half of 3 is 1.5.

y=4\times 1.5=6

6 is half of 12.

y is halved.

Formula: Inverse variation means y=k/x. k stays the same when x changes.

Step 1 · The new x is 2x.

Step 2 · Put that into the formula.

y_{\text{new}}=\dfrac{k}{2x}=\dfrac{1}{2}\times\dfrac{k}{x}=\dfrac{1}{2}y

Step 3 · Check: if y=12 when x=3, then k=36. Double x to 6:

y=\dfrac{36}{6}=6

6 is half of 12.

y is halved.

Same formula: y=k/x. k stays fixed.

Step 1 · The new x is x/2.

Step 2 · Dividing by half is the same as multiplying by 2.

y_{\text{new}}=\dfrac{k}{x/2}=2\times\dfrac{k}{x}=2y

Step 3 · Same check pair: k=36. Half of x=3 is 1.5.

y=\dfrac{36}{1.5}=24

24 is double 12.

y doubles.

4 · Exercise 3I Foundation

y varies inversely with the square of x.

(a) (i) Write this algebraically. Use k for the constant.

(a) (ii) y=2 when x=3. Find k.

(a) (iii) Find y when x=6.

(b) What happens to y when x is:

(i) doubled?   (ii) halved?

Formula: “Inversely with the square” means the square of x is on the bottom. k is the fixed numerator.

y=\dfrac{k}{x^2}.

Formula: y=k/x^2. In this part k is that fixed numerator. The pair is y=2 when x=3, so k=yx^2.

Step 1 · Square the given x first. 3^2=9.

Step 2 · Put the pair in.

2=\dfrac{k}{9}

Step 3 · Multiply both sides by 9.

k=2\times 9=18

k=18.

Same formula: y=k/x^2 with the k=18 just found. Do not find a new k.

Step 1 · Square the new x. 6^2=36.

Step 2 · Divide k by that square.

y=\dfrac{18}{36}=\dfrac{1}{2}

When x=6, y=\dfrac{1}{2}.

Same formula: y=k/x^2. If x doubles, the square becomes four times as big, so y becomes one quarter.

Step 1 · New input 2x. Its square is (2x)^2=4x^2.

Step 2 · The new output is

y_{\text{new}}=\dfrac{k}{4x^2}=\dfrac{1}{4}\times\dfrac{k}{x^2}=\dfrac{1}{4}y

Step 3 · Check with this question’s pair. x=3, y=2, k=18. Double x to 6, which is part (a)(iii):

y=\dfrac{1}{2}

One half is one quarter of 2.

y becomes one quarter of its old value.

Same formula: y=k/x^2. If x is halved, the square becomes one quarter, so y becomes four times as big.

Step 1 · New input x/2. Its square is x^2/4.

Step 2 · Dividing by a quarter multiplies by 4.

y_{\text{new}}=\dfrac{k}{x^2/4}=4\times\dfrac{k}{x^2}=4y

Step 3 · Check: half of x=3 is 1.5. 1.5^2=2.25. Use k=18.

y=\dfrac{18}{2.25}=8

8 is four times 2. (Because 2.25\times 8=18.)

y becomes four times its old value.

5 · Exercise 3I Foundation

The area A cm² of a triangle varies directly with its height h cm.

(a) Write this algebraically. Use k for the constant.

(b) Find k if A=12 cm² when h=3 cm.

(c) What are the units of k?

(d) Explain those units using the area formula for a triangle.

Formula: Varies directly means A=kh. Here k is the fixed multiplier that turns the height into the area.

A=kh.

Formula: A=kh. In this part k is that multiplier. The pair is A=12 when h=3, so k=A/h.

Step 1 · Put the pair in.

12=k\times 3

Step 2 · Divide both sides by 3.

k=\dfrac{12}{3}=4

k=4.

Formula: From A=kh, k=A/h. Units follow that division.

A is in cm². h is in cm.

cm² divided by cm is cm. One centimetre cancels.

The units of k are centimetres.

Formula: Area of a triangle is A=\dfrac{1}{2}bh. The base b and the height h are both lengths. The \dfrac{1}{2} has no units.

Step 1 · If the base stays the same and only the height changes, write

A=\left(\dfrac{1}{2}b\right)h

Step 2 · Compare with A=kh. The bracket is the constant:

k=\dfrac{1}{2}b

Step 3 · b is a length in centimetres, so \dfrac{1}{2}b is also in centimetres. That matches part (c).

k is half the base, measured in centimetres.

In a scale model of a Spitfire, each length m on the model varies directly with the matching length s on the real aircraft.

(a) Write this algebraically.

(b) The real aircraft is 9.12 m long. The model is 0.38 m long. Find the constant of proportionality.

(c) The real aircraft is 3.48 m high on the runway. How high is the model?

(d) The model’s wingspan is 468 mm. What is the real wingspan, in metres?

Formula: Varies directly means m=ks. Here k is the scale factor: model length divided by real length.

m=ks.

Formula: m=ks. In this part k is the scale factor. The pair is m=0.38 when s=9.12, both in metres, so k=m/s.

Step 1 · Put the pair in.

0.38=k\times 9.12

Step 2 · Divide both sides by 9.12. Multiply top and bottom by 100 to clear the decimals.

k=\dfrac{0.38}{9.12}=\dfrac{38}{912}

Step 3 · Divide top and bottom by 2.

k=\dfrac{19}{456}

Step 4 · 456\div 19=24, so

k=\dfrac{19}{456}=\dfrac{1}{24}

k=\dfrac{1}{24}.

Same formula: m=ks with k=\dfrac{1}{24} from part (b). Here s=3.48 m is the real height. m is the model height.

Step 1 · Substitute. Do not find a new k.

m=\dfrac{1}{24}\times 3.48=\dfrac{3.48}{24}

Step 2 · 24\times 0.145=3.48, so the division is 0.145.

Step 3 · In millimetres, 0.145\times 1000=145.

The model is 0.145 m high, which is 145 mm.

Same formula: m=ks with k=\dfrac{1}{24}. This time m is known and s is the unknown. k was found in metres, so change 468 mm into metres first.

Step 1 · 468 mm =468\div 1000=0.468 m.

Step 2 · Put the model length in.

0.468=\dfrac{1}{24}s

Step 3 · Multiply both sides by 24.

s=0.468\times 24

Step 4 · Split 24 as 20 + 4.

0.468\times 20=9.36

0.468\times 4=1.872

s=9.36+1.872=11.232

The real wingspan is 11.232 m.

k=\dfrac{1}{12}

So the rule is P=\dfrac{1}{12}A. The graph is a straight line through the origin. The known point is (576,\,48).

Horizontal axis: area A in m². Vertical axis: paint P in litres. The dashed guides meet at the given pair. They stop before the tick numbers.

k=\dfrac{1}{12}, and the graph is the line above.

Same formula: P=kA with k=\dfrac{1}{12} from part (b). The new area is A=668.

Step 1 · Substitute. Do not find a new k.

P=\dfrac{1}{12}\times 668=\dfrac{668}{12}

Step 2 · Divide top and bottom by 4.

P=\dfrac{167}{3}=55\dfrac{2}{3}

Step 3 · 55\dfrac{2}{3}=55.666\ldots, which is 55.7 L to one decimal place.

55\dfrac{2}{3} litres, about 55.7 L.

Formula: Fixed area means \ell b=48, so b=48/\ell. The sides vary inversely. Here the constant is k=48.

Step 1 · List whole-number factor pairs of 48. Stop when the pairs repeat in the other order.

1\times 48, 2\times 24, 3\times 16, 4\times 12, 6\times 8.
