Chapter 3 · Functions and graphs

3I · Direct and inverse variation

Direct path: Concept → Tutorial → Exercise.

What this section is about

Two quantities can stay in a fixed ratio, or they can have a fixed product.

  • Varies directly means y=kx. The graph is a straight line through the origin. k is the fixed multiplier.
  • Concrete pair: if y=12 when x=3, then k=12\div 3=4, so y=4x. Double x from 3 to 6 and y doubles from 12 to 24.
  • Varies inversely means y=k/x. Double x and y is halved. Same pair the other way: k=12\times 3=36, so y=36/x. Double x to 6 and y becomes 6, which is half of 12.
  • Order of work: always find k from the given pair. Then use that same k to find the new value.
  • Powers: the link can be a square, a cube, or a square root. There is still one constant k. Find it first.

Full detail and tiny worked examples live in the Tutorial · theory. This page only sets the scene.

Prior knowledge — quick notes

Keep these in mind. The Tutorial shows each one with numbers.

  • Substitute: replace the letter with the number, then calculate.
  • A line through the origin is y=kx. There is no extra “start” number added on.
  • Undo a multiply by dividing. Undo a divide by multiplying.
  • If a number doubles, its square becomes four times as big. Half the number, and the square becomes one quarter.
  • A square root is the number that multiplies by itself to give the inside.
