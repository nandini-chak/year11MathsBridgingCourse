3E · Completing the square and the graph

Direct path: Concept → Tutorial → Exercise.

What this section is about

Completing the square rewrites a quadratic so the vertex can be read straight off the formula.

  • Monic first: if x^2 has coefficient 1, halve the middle number, square that half, then add and subtract it.
  • Non-monic next: if the number in front of x^2 is not 1, factor that number out, then do the monic steps inside the bracket.
  • Read the vertex: y=a(x-h)^2+k has axis x=h and vertex (h,k).
  • A square is never negative: the parabola turns at the vertex and does not cross to the other side of it.
  • Zeroes: put y=0. You may get two x-intercepts, one double zero, or none.

Tiny worked examples, including one labelled parabola, are in the Tutorial · theory.

Prior knowledge — quick notes

Keep these in mind. The Tutorial uses each one with numbers.

  • Expand: (x+3)^2=x^2+6x+9. The middle term is twice the number inside the bracket.
  • Halve means divide by 2. Half of 6 is 3. Half of -4 is -2. Half of 1 is \dfrac12.
  • A square is 0 or positive: 4^2=16 and (-4)^2=16.
  • The y-intercept is the output when x=0.
  • If the number in front of x^2 is positive, the parabola opens upward. If it is negative, the parabola opens downward.
